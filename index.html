About KGN Engineering Works

YOUR LOCAL PARTNER IN QUALITY FABRICATION

Based in Laheriasarai, Darbhanga, KGN Engineering Works is a one-stop destination for iron and steel manufacturing, fabrication, and welding services. We specialise in trailer and water tanker manufacturing, iron gates, grills, shutters, and custom steel products.

We also provide on-site welding and convenient assistance with vehicle-related services. Our goal is dependable workmanship and practical solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial customers.
